PHOTOS FOR THE BIRTHDAY ROOM FRAME
==================================

Put your pictures in this folder. They appear one at a time in the gold
frame on the wall of the final birthday room, changing every 2 seconds
and looping forever.

The frame is landscape, so wide photos fit best. Taller photos are
cropped neatly to fill the frame.

Because the game runs straight from your disk (no server), the browser
cannot look inside this folder by itself. Choose ONE of these:

  A) EASIEST: number the files
     Name the pictures 1.jpg, 2.jpg, 3.jpg ... (png, webp and gif work too).
     They play in that order. Don't skip numbers.

     Shortcut: double-click  number-my-photos.bat  in this folder and it
     renames every picture here to 1, 2, 3 ... for you (keeping the
     file type). Keep a copy of your originals elsewhere if the names matter.

  B) Keep your own file names
     Open js/config.js and list them in the photos setting, in order:
         photos: ['beach.jpg', 'birthday-2025.png', 'the-cats.jpg'],

To change the speed, edit  photoSeconds: 2  in js/config.js.

With no photos, the frame shows a small "Happy memories" picture instead.
