PHOTOS FOR THE MEMORY BOOK
==========================

Put the pictures in the year folders, named like this:
    2022/photo-01.jpg   2022/photo-02.jpg   2022/photo-03.jpg
    2023/photo-01.jpg ... and so on, up to 2026
    cats/cat-01.jpg     cats/cat-02.jpg     (the two new cats)

The file names, captions, years and titles are all listed in
    js/memories-data.js
so you can use any names you like, as long as they match that list.
Add or remove photos there too (2 to 6 per year look best).

Until a picture exists, the book shows a soft placeholder with the file
name on it (and a little drawing for each cat), so nothing ever breaks.

Big photos are fine: they are cropped to fit the Polaroids, and only the
pages near the one Anne is reading are loaded.

textures/ and sounds/ are spare folders: the book currently draws its
paper and makes its sounds by itself, so they are not needed.
